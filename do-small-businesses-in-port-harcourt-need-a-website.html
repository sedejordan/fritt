Do small businesses in Port Harcourt need a website?

By Jordan Sede, Fritt. Updated October 2026.

The short answer: not every small business needs one, but most benefit from having one. If customers find you through word of mouth alone and you are already busy, you can manage without a website for now. If you want new customers to find you, trust you, and contact you easily, a simple website is one of the most useful things you can have.

When you can manage without a website

  • Nearly all your customers come from referrals and you are already at full capacity.
  • You sell a very small range and take orders in person or by phone.
  • You are testing an idea and are not ready to invest yet.

Even then, set up a free Google Business Profile so people searching for your business can find your address, phone number, and opening hours.

When you need a website

  • Customers check you online before they call or visit. If they cannot find anything, some will choose a competitor who is easier to check.
  • You want customers you have never met. People who search for your service can only find you if you appear somewhere searchable.
  • You want to look credible. Companies, schools, and organisations often expect a business to have a website before they work with it.
  • You send the same details again and again. A website holds your services, prices, location, and contact details in one place, so you stop repeating them in chats.
  • You want to sell online. An online store needs a website. See ecommerce website development in Port Harcourt.

Is social media enough?

Social media is a good way to show your work and talk to customers, and you should keep using it. But your pages sit on a platform you do not control, the rules and reach can change, and posts disappear down the feed. A website is an address that you own. It stays the same, it can be found through search, and it can hold the information customers look for first.

What a small business website should include

  • What you do, in plain words, on the first screen.
  • Your services, and prices where you can share them.
  • Your location, a map, and your opening hours.
  • Clear ways to contact you, especially a WhatsApp button and a call button.
  • Photos of your real work, premises, or products.
  • A design that works properly on a phone, since most visitors will use one.
