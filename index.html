FAQ

Common questions

How much does a website cost in Port Harcourt?

A Business Website starts at ₦300,000 for up to 5 pages, and an Ecommerce Starter store starts at ₦500,000 for up to 20 products. These are fixed starting prices. The final quote depends on your pages, products, and features, and I send a short written scope, timeline, and fixed price before any work begins. Both include your domain and standard hosting for the first year.

Are the domain and hosting included in the price?

Yes. Both packages include your domain and standard hosting for the first year. After that, you pay the renewals. Optional monthly maintenance does not include hosting.

How long does it take to build a website?

A Business Website takes 7 to 10 working days. An Ecommerce Starter store takes 14 to 21 working days. The exact timeline is confirmed in the written scope before we start.

What is included in the Business Website package?

Up to 5 pages, a mobile responsive design, a contact form with WhatsApp and call buttons, Google Maps, basic SEO setup, one round of revisions, training so you can edit your content yourself, and your domain and standard hosting for the first year.

Can you build an ecommerce store for my business?

Yes. The Ecommerce Starter package includes up to 20 products, a cart, checkout and payments, delivery options, two rounds of revisions, training so you can manage products and orders yourself, and your domain and standard hosting for the first year.

How do payments work on an online store?

Customers pay at checkout through a payment provider connected to your own account, so the money goes straight to you. For Nigerian businesses my default is Flutterwave, and I can set up Paystack or another provider if you prefer. Businesses registered in a country Stripe supports can use Stripe.

Will I be able to update the website myself?

Yes. Every package includes training so you can edit content on your own, and I stay available for 30 days after launch. If you would rather not handle it, optional monthly maintenance from ₦25,000 covers updates, backups, security, and small edits. It does not cover hosting renewals, which are paid separately after the first year.

Do you work with clients outside Port Harcourt?

Yes. I’m based in Nigeria and work with clients locally and remotely, in Nigeria and abroad. WhatsApp works from anywhere, so we can start the conversation whenever suits you.
